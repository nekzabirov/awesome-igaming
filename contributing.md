# Contribution Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Awesome iGaming.

Please ensure your pull request adheres to the following guidelines:

## Submission rules

- **Search previous suggestions** before submitting to avoid duplicates.
- **One pull request per suggestion.** This makes review easier and isolates discussion.
- **Format:** `[Project Name](link) - Description.`
- **Description style:**
  - Start with a capital letter.
  - End with a full stop.
  - Keep it concise (one or two sentences) and concrete. Say what the project does, not how awesome it is.
  - Avoid marketing language.
- **Placement:** Add new entries at the bottom of the relevant section unless alphabetical ordering is established for that section.
- **Spelling and grammar:** Run your text through a grammar check.
- **Trailing whitespace:** Make sure your editor strips it.

## What is eligible

- Open-source software for iGaming, online casino, sports betting, poker, bingo, or lottery use cases.
- Game engines, aggregators, lobby backends, math models, RNG/provably-fair libraries, KYC/AML tools used in iGaming, responsible-gambling tooling.
- Public regulatory standards and technical specifications relevant to operators and integrators.
- Articles, talks, and books with substantial technical content for iGaming engineers.

## What is not eligible

- Affiliate sites, casino review portals, or marketing material.
- Closed-source commercial products without a free tier or open documentation worth referencing.
- Hobby projects without a maintained README or any usage signals.
- Scammy "100% profit" betting bots, arbitrage tools that violate operator terms, or anything promoting fraud.
- Personal blog posts without technical substance.

## What makes a strong submission

- Public repository with a meaningful README.
- Permissive or open-source license.
- Recent activity (commits in the last 12 months) OR an explanation in your PR for why an older project is still relevant.
- At least one of: production use, non-trivial scope, or a unique angle the list does not yet cover.

## Quality bar

This list aims to be useful to engineers actually building or auditing iGaming systems. Quality over quantity. Maintainers may decline submissions that do not meet the bar even if they technically fit a category.

## Pull request checklist

- [ ] Project fits one of the existing categories (or proposes a new one with at least two entries).
- [ ] Entry follows the format above.
- [ ] Description is accurate and free of marketing language.
- [ ] Link is correct and reachable.
- [ ] No duplicates.
- [ ] Spelling and grammar checked.

Thanks for helping curate the iGaming open-source landscape.
